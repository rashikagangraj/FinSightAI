# S&P 500 Sector Overview — 2023 Performance Summary

## Index Performance

The S&P 500 gained approximately **24.2%** in 2023, recovering sharply from the -18.1% decline in 2022.
The rally was concentrated in large-cap technology stocks, particularly those with AI exposure.

## Key Macro Themes in 2023

**Artificial Intelligence Supercycle**
The launch of ChatGPT in late 2022 and subsequent AI product announcements drove massive
investment in AI infrastructure. NVIDIA's revenue grew 122% in fiscal 2024 (ending Jan 2024),
with data center revenue hitting $47.5 billion. The "Magnificent Seven" — Apple, Microsoft,
Amazon, Alphabet, Meta, NVIDIA, Tesla — collectively contributed approximately 65% of the
S&P 500's total return.

**Federal Reserve Policy**
The Fed raised rates to a peak 5.25-5.50% target range in July 2023, the highest in 22 years.
Rate pause signals in Q4 2023 triggered a year-end rally, with markets pricing in 150bps of
cuts for 2024.

**Earnings Resilience**
S&P 500 earnings per share grew approximately 0-2% in 2023, better than feared recession
scenarios. Profit margins proved resilient as companies shed excess pandemic-era headcount
and reduced costs.

## Valuation

S&P 500 forward P/E ratio: approximately 19.5x at year-end 2023
10-year average forward P/E: ~17x
Premium driven by AI optimism and large-cap tech concentration

## Risk Outlook for 2024

- **Higher-for-longer rates**: If inflation re-accelerates, equity multiples compress
- **Commercial real estate**: $1.5 trillion in CRE debt matures in 2024-2025
- **Geopolitical risk**: Middle East conflict, U.S.-China tensions, 2024 election uncertainty
- **AI valuation risk**: Execution must justify lofty expectations priced into tech stocks
- **Credit stress**: Rising corporate defaults as low-rate debt refinanced at higher costs
