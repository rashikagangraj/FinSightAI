RISK FACTORS

The Company faces intense competition in all its markets. Competitors with greater financial
resources, lower prices, or more established brands could adversely affect the Company's business.
The Company is subject to risks related to global supply chain disruptions, geopolitical tensions
particularly between the U.S. and China, and macroeconomic conditions including inflation and
foreign currency fluctuations.

RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT

Research and development expense: $29.9 billion in fiscal 2023
The Company continues to invest heavily in silicon design, machine learning, augmented reality,
and health-related technologies through its custom chip program (Apple Silicon).

CAPITAL ALLOCATION STRATEGY

Apple has returned over $600 billion to shareholders since initiating its capital return program.
The Board of Directors has authorized an additional $90 billion in share repurchases as of
May 2023. The Company's goal is to become net-cash neutral over time.
