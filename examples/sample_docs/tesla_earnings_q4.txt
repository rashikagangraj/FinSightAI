TESLA, INC.
Q4 AND FULL YEAR 2023 EARNINGS CALL TRANSCRIPT
(Excerpted from public investor relations materials)

FULL YEAR 2023 FINANCIAL RESULTS

Total revenue: $97.7 billion, up 19% year-over-year
Automotive revenue: $82.4 billion
Energy generation and storage revenue: $6.0 billion
Services and other revenue: $8.3 billion

Net income (GAAP): $15.0 billion
Net income margin: 15.3%
Adjusted EBITDA: $14.0 billion
Adjusted EBITDA margin: 14.3%

Earnings per share (diluted, GAAP): $4.30
Free cash flow: $4.4 billion
Cash and cash equivalents: $29.1 billion

GROSS MARGIN

Automotive gross margin: 18.9% (Q4 2023), down from 25.9% in Q4 2022
The decline reflects strategic price reductions to maintain volume and market share,
increased depreciation of Gigafactory assets, and cost headwinds from ramping new models.

Total company gross margin: 17.6% (Q4 2023)

DELIVERIES AND PRODUCTION

Total deliveries in 2023: 1,808,581 vehicles, up 38% year-over-year
  - Model 3/Y: 1,739,707 deliveries
  - Model S/X/Cybertruck: 68,874 deliveries

Total production: 1,845,985 vehicles

ENERGY BUSINESS

Energy storage deployed: 14.7 GWh in 2023, a record high
Megapack revenue grew significantly, with strong demand from utility-scale storage projects
Solar deployments: 2.5 GW

BALANCE SHEET (December 31, 2023)

Total assets: $106.6 billion
Total debt: $5.2 billion
Shareholders' equity: $62.6 billion

OPERATING EXPENSES

Research and development: $3.97 billion in 2023
Selling, general and administrative: $4.80 billion in 2023

GUIDANCE AND FORWARD OUTLOOK

Management noted that 2024 growth rate may be notably lower than 2023 due to:
- The company is between two major growth waves
- Higher interest rates reducing consumer purchasing power
- Competitive pricing pressures from Chinese automakers
- Investment in next-generation vehicle platform and AI/autonomous driving

Tesla expects to launch its next-generation vehicle (Cybercab robotaxi) and continue
scaling Autopilot and Full Self-Driving (FSD) capabilities. The FSD price was reduced
from $15,000 to $12,000 in the United States to accelerate adoption.

SUPERCHARGER NETWORK

Number of Supercharger stations: 5,952 globally
Connector opens to other EV brands (Ford, GM, Rivian partnerships announced)
This generates incremental revenue and positions Tesla's NACS connector as the industry standard.
