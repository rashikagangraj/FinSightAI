AIKART TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED
ANNUAL FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS & EARNINGS STATEMENT
Fiscal Year: FY2025–26 (Period Ended March 31, 2026)

1. Executive Financial Summary
AIKART reported strong financial performance for the fiscal year 2025–26 (FY2025–26), driven by robust growth across its digital commerce, AI automation, and enterprise retail intelligence platforms.

2. Key Financial Metrics (FY2025–26):
- Total Revenue: ₹78.0 Cr (up 42% YoY compared to ₹55.0 Cr in FY2024–25)
- Net Profit (Profit After Tax): ₹5.7 Cr (net margin of 7.3%)
- Cash and Cash Equivalents (Cash Balance): ₹25.0 Cr
- Operating Income (EBITDA): ₹9.2 Cr
- Gross Profit Margin: 38.5%
- Total Assets: ₹112.0 Cr
- Total Debt: ₹14.0 Cr
- Shareholders' Equity: ₹68.0 Cr
- Free Cash Flow (FCF): ₹4.2 Cr
- Diluted EPS: ₹14.25 per share

3. Operational & Business Highlights:
- Active transacting merchant partners grew by 65% to 45,000+.
- AI-driven product recommendation engine contributed ₹32.0 Cr to gross platform sales.
- Logistics overhead decreased by 180 bps due to automated regional fulfillment hubs.

4. Balance Sheet & Liquidity:
The company maintains a healthy liquidity cushion with cash balance of ₹25.0 Cr and zero short-term working capital debt. Net debt-to-equity ratio stands conservatively at 0.21x.

5. Risk Factors & Outlook for FY2026–27:
Key operational risks include competitive customer acquisition costs in Tier-2/Tier-3 cities, regulatory compliance in regional retail taxes, and server infrastructure scaling investments.
